HR Knowledge Base

Leave Policy:
Employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year. Leave requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

Recruitment:
All job vacancies must be approved by the hiring manager and HR before posting.

Performance Reviews:
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December.

Employee Benefits:
Employees are eligible for health insurance, pension contributions, and professional development training.

Working Hours:
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
