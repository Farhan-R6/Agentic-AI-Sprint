IT Support Knowledge Base

Password Reset:
Users can reset passwords through the self-service portal or by contacting the IT Service Desk.

Laptop Issues:
Restart the device before submitting a support ticket.

Software Installation:
Only approved software may be installed on company devices.

VPN Access:
Remote employees must use the company VPN to access internal resources.

Security:
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all company accounts.
