Company Policy Handbook

Code of Conduct:
Employees must maintain professionalism and treat colleagues with respect.

Data Protection:
Confidential company and customer information must not be shared without authorization.

Remote Work Policy:
Employees may work remotely with manager approval and must remain reachable during business hours.

Acceptable Use Policy:
Company devices should be used primarily for business purposes.

Compliance:
Employees are required to comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and company policies.
